## 📌 Overview
**Songs Player** is a modern, high-fidelity music streaming and audio management player powered by AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer) and Material Design 3. It gives you the freedom to stream trending music online, download tracks as MP3 audio or MP4 video, organize your local music library, and shape your sound with a professional 5-band equalizer.

---

## 📱 Core Features & User Guide

### 1. 🗂️ Navigation & Modes (4 Tabs)
- **Online Tab:** Discover trending online songs, quick-play recommendations, recently played history, and search across global music catalogues.
- **Artists Tab:** Browse comprehensive artist discographies, top tracks, and albums.
- **Offline Tab:** Organize and play your downloaded and local audio collection by Tracks, Albums, Artists, Playlists, and Genres.
- **Device Tab:** Direct file-system explorer to discover audio files scattered across internal storage and SD cards.

---

### 2. 🎧 Online Streaming & Offline Downloads
- **High-Quality Streaming:** Stream music in up to 320 kbps bitrates with intelligent network caching.
- **Download as MP3 or MP4:** When listening to any track, tap the 3-dots player menu to download either the high-quality **Audio (.mp3)** or the official **Music Video (.mp4)** directly to your local storage.
- **Automatic Metadata Tagging:** Downloaded songs automatically retain title, artist, album art, duration, and track number.

---

### 3. 🎛️ Audio Controls & Full-Screen Player
- **Persistent Mini Player:** Floats above all screens with instant Play/Pause, Next Track, Favorite Star toggle, and progress bar. Tap anywhere on the mini player to open the full experience.
- **Full Player Experience:**
  - **Animated Artwork:** Rotating vinyl disc with embedded album artwork.
  - **Seek Bar & Time Stamps:** Smooth scrubbing with real-time buffered stream indicators.
  - **Repeat & Shuffle:** Switch between *Repeat All*, *Repeat One*, and *Shuffle Queue*.
  - **Playback Speed:** Adjust playback tempo from 0.5x up to 2.0x without pitch distortion.

---

### 4. 🎚️ 5-Band Equalizer & Audio Effects
- **5-Band Frequency Equalizer:** Fine-tune audio bands for custom acoustic tuning.
- **Genre Presets:** Normal, Classical, Dance, Flat, Heavy Metal, Hip Hop, Jazz, Pop, Rock, and Custom.
- **Bass Boost:** Deep low-frequency subwoofer amplifier.
- **3D Virtualizer:** Spatial surround sound simulation for headphones and external speakers.
- **Loudness Enhancer:** Boost dynamic headroom for quieter audio tracks.

---

### 5. ⏱️ Sleep Timer
- **Smart Countdown:** Set sleep timers for 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 minutes.
- **Gentle Fadeout:** Gradually eases audio playback to a stop when the timer expires so your sleep remains undisturbed.

---

### 6. 📜 Playlists & Smart Collections
- **Custom Playlists:** Create, rename, edit, and organize custom music playlists.
- **Smart System Playlists:**
  - ⭐ **Favorites:** Instant access to all your starred songs.
  - 🕒 **Recently Played:** Keeps track of your listening history.
  - 🔥 **Most Played:** Auto-ranks your most frequently listened tracks.
  - 🆕 **Recently Added:** Highlights newly scanned or downloaded audio.

---

### 7. ⚙️ Settings & Audio Preferences
- **Material You Dynamic Theming:** Automatically adapts theme accents to match your device wallpaper (Android 12+).
- **Gapless Playback:** Eliminates annoying pauses between consecutive album tracks.
- **Crossfade:** Smoothly fades out the outgoing song while fading in the next.
- **Streaming Quality Selector:** High (320 kbps), Medium (192 kbps), or Low (128 kbps) to conserve mobile data.
- **Cache Cleaner:** One-tap button to clear temporary thumbnail and audio chunk caches.
